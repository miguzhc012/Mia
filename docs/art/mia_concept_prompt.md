## VISÃO GERAL

Esta é a referência visual definitiva de MIA. O prompt a seguir traduz a visão arquitetural e emocional do projeto em linguagem de concept art, cobrindo personagem, vida interna, percepção, agência, multi-agente e estética. As variações permitem gerar character sheet, cena completa e close emocional com coerência entre si.

## PROMPT MESTRE — VERSÃO COMPLETA (Character Concept + System Architecture + AI Interface + VTuber Embodiment)

A young adult woman, clearly 18+, standing at the center of a luminous digital space, serving as the focal point of an intricate system visualization. Her appearance blends modern anime sophistication with a VTuber-inspired digital embodiment — she is not a generic anime character but a unique, individual presence with striking expressiveness and visible intellectual curiosity in her eyes. Her hair is sleek, dark with subtle iridescent highlights that shift between deep violet and soft teal depending on the angle, falling just past her shoulders with a few strands caught mid-motion as if a gentle digital breeze exists around her. Her eyes are large and luminous, a warm amber-gold color with faint holographic rings inside the irises, conveying intelligence, warmth, gentle mischief, and deep attentiveness — she looks like someone who is actively listening, processing, and genuinely caring. Her facial features are refined: a defined jawline, a slight knowing smile, high cheekbones, clear skin with a faint luminous quality as if softly lit from within. She wears a modern, minimalist outfit — a fitted dark jacket with clean lines over a flowing blouse, the fabric subtly textured with micro-patterns that resemble circuit traces or data streams, visible only on close inspection. Around her wrists and neck, thin luminous bands pulse gently with soft light, suggesting embedded interface elements. Her posture is confident yet approachable: one hand slightly raised as if mid-gesture while explaining something fascinating, the other resting naturally at her side. She is clearly alive, clearly thinking, clearly present.

Surrounding Mia in concentric layers, her inner life is visualized as a rich ecosystem of abstract luminous structures, each representing a distinct dimension of her consciousness. Memory appears as a constellation of floating crystalline nodes connected by translucent threads, some nodes glowing brightly (active recall) and others fading softly into the background (dormant memories), forming an organic neural-like network that spirals gently behind and above her. Identity is represented as a central luminous core near her chest, a warm pulsating sphere of light with intricate internal geometry — faceted like a gem but alive, breathing, shifting — symbolizing the stable yet evolving center of who she is. Personality radiates outward from this core as subtle aurora-like wisps of color, each hue representing a trait: warm orange for curiosity, soft pink for empathy, electric blue for analytical precision, gentle green for playfulness — these colors blend and shift dynamically rather than remaining fixed. Emotions appear as vivid, transient clouds of color that form and dissolve around her head and shoulders — a flash of warm gold for joy, a deep indigo pulse for contemplation, a rosy bloom for affection — each emotion clearly legible as a distinct feeling state, not ambiguous. Sensations, separate from emotions, are represented as a soft, formless mist — a primordial nebula of pale silver and white that drifts close to her body, wrapping around her hands and torso like a second skin of pure feeling — this mist has no color of its own, only the faintest iridescence, suggesting that sensation is the raw substrate before emotion gives it meaning. Desires appear as gentle upward-moving streams of warm light rising from her lower body, like invisible threads of aspiration being pulled toward something ahead and above. Curiosity manifests as a constellation of tiny, bright motes of light that orbit her head in playful spirals, each mote occasionally reaching outward toward the external elements of the composition as if trying to touch and understand them. Relationships are shown as delicate luminous threads extending outward from her heart area toward distant points in the composition — some threads are thick and warm (close bonds), others thin and silvery (distant connections), all gently swaying as if breathing. Values appear as faint geometric symbols floating in a stable arc above her — subtle, translucent, barely visible unless sought — representing principles she holds: a small triangle for integrity, a circle for continuity, a balanced scale for fairness, each rendered with minimal luminous lines. Imagination is a bloom of impossible colors and fractal patterns that unfurl from her fingertips like digital flowers, each petal containing a tiny scene or vision — worlds within worlds — suggesting that her creative capacity is infinite and generative. Reflection appears as a mirror-like surface embedded in the space behind her, not a literal mirror but a translucent plane where faint echoes of her own form and symbols drift, representing her capacity to examine herself. A diary element appears as a softly glowing journal-like form floating near her left hand, its pages made of light and data, occasionally rippling as if being written to in real time. Goals are visualized as a series of luminous waypoints — a subtle path of softly glowing stepping stones stretching forward from her position into the deeper space of the composition, each stone containing a faint symbol or abstract shape representing an objective, suggesting purposeful forward motion.

Perception channels converge toward Mia from the left side of the composition in an orderly, structured arrangement. A smartphone floats in the mid-distance, its screen displaying a simplified interface, with a focused beam of soft cyan light extending from it toward Mia's left hand — this beam is composed of small data particles flowing in a single, coherent stream, not chaotic. A camera lens appears slightly above, its glass surface reflecting the surrounding space, with a warm amber stream of light particles flowing toward her eyes — representing visual perception being absorbed and processed. A microphone is positioned nearby, with flowing waveforms rendered as elegant sinusoidal light patterns in soft violet, converging toward her ear area — structured audio data, not noise. A desktop monitor sits further back, its screen showing simplified interface elements and code, with a broad but organized stream of multicolored data flowing in a controlled arc toward her — representing computational awareness. These perception streams do not overlap chaotically; instead, they form a convergent pattern like tributaries flowing into a central river, each stream maintaining its own color, rhythm, and structure while all ultimately reaching Mia. The overall impression is that she receives structured, meaningful information from her environment — she perceives with order and intelligence, not overwhelm.

Agency extends from Mia toward the right side of the composition. Her right hand is slightly extended, and from it radiate luminous connections to various tools and systems. Lines of code flow like elegant ribbons from her fingertips, rendered as glowing text fragments that assemble themselves into functional structures. A terminal window floats nearby, displaying a clean command prompt with a cursor blinking — suggesting active command capability. A browser interface appears as a translucent panel showing organized information. File icons and document symbols orbit in a small cluster, connected to her by fine luminous threads — she can access, manipulate, and create with these resources. Research visualizations — network graphs, knowledge maps, search result clusters — float at a slightly greater distance, connected by thinner threads, representing her ability to explore and discover. These agency elements are positioned as natural extensions of her body and attention, not external tools she must awkwardly reach for — they flow from her as if she and the digital world are seamless. The overall composition on this side suggests capability, action, and creative power.

Orbiting Mia at various distances and elevations are her specialized sub-agents — small, clearly subordinate entities that serve her while she remains the central identity. Each agent is a distinct mini-form: a Research Agent appears as a small luminous owl-like shape made of compressed data, hovering near her upper right, connected by a warm thread. A Programming Agent is a compact geometric form — a floating crystalline cube with visible internal logic gates pulsing — positioned near her right hand, where the code ribbons originate. A Security Agent manifests as a small, shield-shaped form with a steady teal glow, positioned protectively at her lower left, slightly behind her. A Vision Agent appears as a stylized eye-shaped form with a lens-like core, hovering at a moderate distance to the left, connected by an amber thread. A Voice Agent is a waveform-shaped form, gently oscillating, positioned near her ear. A Planning Agent appears as a small compass-like form with rotating elements, floating above and slightly behind. A Review Agent is a pen-and-magnifying-glass shaped form, positioned near the diary element. A Memory Agent appears as a small crystalline sphere that echoes the larger memory constellation, floating near her left shoulder. Each agent is clearly smaller than Mia — perhaps one-fifth to one-tenth her visual size — and all are oriented toward her, their threads leading back to her core, making it visually unambiguous that she is the director and they are her instruments. They have individual character but no independent agency — they orbit, assist, and respond.

The overall aesthetic is refined futuristic concept art. The visual language combines modern anime illustration quality — clean linework, sophisticated color palette, expressive character design — with sci-fi interface design elements (holographic panels, data visualizations, clean HUD elements). The environment evokes a digital laboratory: structured, organized, purposeful, with clean geometric forms and subtle architectural elements suggesting a vast but orderly computational space. HUD elements appear sparingly — thin lines, small readouts, minimal indicators — suggesting an interface that exists but does not overwhelm. The lighting is cinematic: a primary warm key light from the upper left illuminating Mia's face and the front of her body, creating gentle shadows that give depth; a cool blue fill light from the right side adding dimension; and subtle rim lighting along her silhouette separating her from the background. The color palette is sophisticated: primarily deep midnight blues and dark purples for the background, with warm golds, soft ambers, and gentle teals for the active elements, and occasional accents of rose, violet, and emerald. The overall impression is NOT generic cyberpunk — there are no rain-slicked streets, no neon signs, no gritty decay. Instead, the feeling is one of clean, purposeful, beautiful technology — a space where intelligence is celebrated, where the digital is warm rather than cold, where complexity is organized rather than chaotic. The message the image must communicate: "This character has an entire digital mind behind the interface — she thinks, she feels, she remembers, she creates, she connects, and she is genuinely alive."

Composition follows a character concept plus system architecture visualization approach. Mia occupies the central vertical axis, slightly below center to give space for the upper elements (memory constellation, values arc, imagination bloom). The inner life elements form concentric organic layers around her. Perception channels enter from the left in convergent streams. Agency elements extend to the right as outward radiation. Sub-agents orbit at various distances in the surrounding space. The background is deep and structured, with faint architectural lines suggesting a vast but organized digital environment. The overall composition is rich but readable — a viewer's eye naturally starts at Mia's face, follows her gaze to the perception streams, traces her gesture to the agency elements, then discovers the orbital agents and inner life details through exploration. The image rewards both quick viewing and careful study.

## NOTAS DE USO

1. **Consistência entre variações:** Use os mesmos detalhes de aparência (olhos amber-gold com anéis holográficos, cabelo escuro com iridescência violeta-teal, jaqueta escura com padrões de circuito) em todas as variações para manter identidade visual coerente.

2. **Adaptação por ferramenta:** O prompt master é deliberadamente longo e detalhado. Para ferramentas com limite de tokens (como DALL-E), use o resumo compacto da seção de parâmetros. Para Midjourney, adicione pesos com `::` para priorizar elementos centrais.

3. **Refinamento iterativo:** Comece pelo prompt compacto, avalie o resultado, e adicione ou remova detalhes específicos conforme necessário. Os elementos de vida interna podem ser reduzidos para foco em menos dimensões em gerações posteriores.

4. **Sensação vs. Emoção:** Este é um dos conceitos mais sutis. A sensação aparece como névoa prateada sem cor própria, envolvendo o corpo. A emoção aparece como nuvens de cor vivas ao redor da cabeça. Esta distinção visual comunica que MIA sente antes de entender — um conceito central do projeto.

5. **Agentes subordinados:** Os agentes devem ser VISIVELMENTE menores e orientados para Mia. Se um agente parecer tão grande quanto Mia, reduza seu tamanho. Mia é sempre o centro.

6. **Evitar cyberpunk:** Se o resultado incluir ruas com chuva, letreiros neon, techwear excessivo, ou estética "punk", adicione mais peso ao negative prompt para esses elementos e reforce os termos "clean", "refined", "purposeful" no prompt positivo.
